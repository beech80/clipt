# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

## Introduction

This policy explains how CLIPT handles information when you use its social video and streaming service. Contact [beecher@cliptgaming.com](mailto:beecher@cliptgaming.com) for privacy, support or safety requests. Never send passwords, verification codes or payment credentials by email.

## Data We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- Username
- Profile information
- Authentication data

### Content Data
- Posts
- Comments
- Streams
- Messages
- Gaming activity

### Safety and Support Data
- Reports, moderation decisions and support correspondence
- Records needed to investigate abuse, fraud or account-security problems

### Technical Data
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Service-performance, request and error logs

### Billing Data Where Applicable
- Subscription status, provider identifiers, transaction and dispute records
- Sandbox transactions are tests. Returning from checkout alone does not confirm payment or membership. Payment details entered at a provider's checkout are handled by that provider under its own notice.

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide and maintain our service
- To notify you about changes
- To provide customer support
- To detect and prevent fraud
- To improve our services

## Data Storage and Security

CLIPT uses service providers including Supabase for database/authentication services and Vercel for web hosting, alongside storage, media-delivery and other providers needed for enabled features. Providers process information needed to deliver their services and may process it in countries other than yours. Access controls and other safeguards help protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Visibility and Sharing

Content you publish may be visible to others according to the destination and visibility of the feature you use. Public posts and profiles can be viewed or copied by other people. Messages and restricted content are processed to provide the service and can require authorized support or moderation access; they are not represented as end-to-end encrypted.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to respond to legal requirements, protect people and the service, investigate abuse or resolve disputes. Connected services and external links may have separate privacy notices. Do not publish someone else's private information without authority.

## Browser Storage and Permissions

CLIPT uses browser storage for sign-in sessions and preferences, including accessibility and controller settings. Clearing it can sign you out or reset local preferences. Camera, microphone and file access depend on the feature you choose and your browser/device permissions. Manage permissions in your browser or device settings; denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

## Retention and Account Deletion

Account information is kept while needed to operate your account and for the purposes described above. Request deletion through the [Delete account page](/delete-account). The app requires confirmation and reports completion only after server-confirmed cleanup. Pending subscriptions, disputes, provider media, shared resources or holds can require review before deletion completes.

The existing retention system keeps minimized financial/accounting records for 7 years by default and security/abuse records for 2 years by default. Periods can change for applicable requirements. Active disputes, chargebacks, fraud investigations and legal holds can extend retention until resolved and the applicable period expires. Restricted retained records exclude unnecessary profile details, passwords and stream/payment credentials.

Deletion cannot recall copies other people downloaded or shared. Backup and external-provider copies may follow separate retention or cleanup processes and are not guaranteed to disappear immediately.

## Your Rights

You can edit available profile information, change preferences and request deletion in the app. Settings includes an account-data export; the current export is not a complete archive and does not include all media or stream credentials. Contact us for additional access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, including rights under the law that applies to you. Identity verification may be necessary before releasing or changing private account information.

## Age Requirements

CLIPT is not intended for children under 13 or anyone below the minimum age required in their location. Users under 18 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian, as described in the [Terms of Service](/terms). If you believe a child below the permitted age provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate.
